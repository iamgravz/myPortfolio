    Case study: Barangay Health Center Management System

    The problem

    A school project. Barangay health centers often keep patient information in logbooks and folders. Staff copy the same details into several records for every visit, and finding a patient's history takes time.

    The solution

    • Patient records and consultations in one place
    • Completing a consultation creates the patient's health record automatically
    • Responsive layout for desktop and tablet

    What I focused on

    Designing the flow so each step feeds the next: the data entered at check-in carries through to the consultation and into the health record without retyping. The project includes a review of related literature on health information systems from 2021 onward.
